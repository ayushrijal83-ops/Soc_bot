Soc_bot

A desktop application that allows users to publish their own prepared content to connected social media accounts using official platform APIs.

Soc_bot runs on your own computer. You connect your accounts through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth), choose a video and caption you have prepared, and Soc_bot uploads them to the accounts you select. It does not create content, and it does not run any server of its own.
